Payment

All purchases — one-time unlocks and subscriptions — are made through Apple's In-App Purchase system and billed to your Apple ID. Apple collects and processes your payment information directly; we never see or store your card or Apple ID billing details. We receive confirmation of your purchase from Apple (the product bought and a transaction identifier) so we can grant the access you paid for.

Connections and what other people can see

Your strengths profile is private by default. When you accept a connection request, the relationship tag you assign — Family, Partner, Friend, Coworker, or Business Partner — determines how much of your profile that person can see. Family and Partner connections see your full 28-trait profile. Friend, Coworker, and Business Partner connections see only your top 10 individual traits; your four domain scores are always visible to any accepted connection. You can review your connections and what they're tagged as inside the app.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, results, connections, chat history) at any time directly in the app — Profile → Delete account. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. You can also reach us at dampiljr.joelg11@gmail.com if you'd rather request deletion by email.

Children

Salamin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
