Privacy Policy — Salamin

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Who we are

Infiniscale, contactable at dampiljr.joelg11@gmail.com, operates the Salamin mobile app ("the app", "we", "us").

What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressAccount creation and sign-in
Display name (optional)Shown to your connections and in the app
Your reflection answers and chat messages Processed to generate your 34-trait strengths profile and to power the Predict/Verify/Present chat features. Sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) for processing — see "Third parties" below.
Your computed strengths profile (34-trait ranking, domain scores) Powers your dashboard, and is shared with people you accept as connections (per the relationship tag you assign them — Family/Partner see more than Friend).
Connections data (who you're connected to, relationship tag, request status) Powers the Connections and Compare features.
Camera access Only used to scan another user's connection code (a QR code). We do not access your camera roll, and no photo or video is stored — the camera feed is only used live, on your device, to read the code.
Usage metadata (message counts, AI token usage) Used to enforce fair-use limits and for our own cost monitoring. Not sold or shared.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos/videos, or any advertising identifiers, and we do not run advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Payment

Unlocking full results or subscribing happens through an external web checkout outside the app. We do not receive or store your card details — that's handled entirely by our payment processor.

Connections and what other people can see

Your strengths profile is private by default. When you accept a connection request, the relationship tag you assign (Family, Partner, or Friend) determines how much of your profile that person can see — Family and Partner connections see your full profile; Friend connections see a limited view. You can review your connections and what they were tagged as inside the app.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, results, connections, chat history) at any time directly in the app — Profile → Delete account. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. You can also reach us at dampiljr.joelg11@gmail.com if you'd rather request deletion by email.

Children

Salamin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
