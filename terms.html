Salamin is a self-reflection tool. It generates a 28-trait strengths profile from your answers to a guided reflection, and offers AI-assisted chat features (Predict future, Verify past, Help with present) and a way to compare your profile with people you connect with.

The reflection and AI responses reflect patterns in how you describe yourself and your situations — they are not a clinical assessment. If you're going through something serious, please talk to a qualified professional.

Connections are a Premium feature. You're responsible for choosing appropriately and for who you connect with. You may group your connections and attach a person or group to an AI chat; the AI is limited to what each person has chosen to share with you.

If your Premium subscription ends, your connections are kept but only their names remain visible; opening their profiles and using them in chat is unavailable until you subscribe again.
